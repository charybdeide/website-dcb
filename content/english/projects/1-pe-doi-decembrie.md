Diaspora Civica Berlin invites you on Saturday, December 2 to resume pre-pandemic customs and traditions and celebrate Romani's national day.

![📍](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/t6b/3/16/1f4cd.png) Location: Flutgraben e.V, Am Flutgraben 3, 12435 Berlin (barrier free)
![🧩](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/t43/3/16/1f9e9.png) The afternoon will start with a series of activities and workshops for parents and little ones, organised by our partners "[Biblioteca Prichindeilor](https://www.facebook.com/prichindeiberlin)" from 14:00 -18:00.
![✨](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/tf6/3/16/2728.png) And from 18:30 three well-known artists on the Berlin and Romanian art scene will turn the evening into a traditional neo-fusion.
![🎶](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/t5d/3/16/1f3b6.png) [Dj Dokia](https://www.facebook.com/dokia3310) will add rhythm to the event, ![🎨](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/tc0/3/16/1f3a8.png) and [#kitra ](https://www.facebook.com/kitrart)and [#passtrada](https://www.facebook.com/passtrada) will entertain the atmosphere with a live drawing act until late into the night.

![🎟️](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/t16/3/16/1f39f.png) Admission: free.
![🍹](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/t2b/3/16/1f379.png) Drinks and snacks will complement the relaxed atmosphere.
![✅](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/images/emoji.php/v9/t35/3/16/2705.png) Everyone is welcome

You can read more detailed descriptions of the artists and future updates on the [Facebook event](https://fb.me/e/1703iSzjF).
