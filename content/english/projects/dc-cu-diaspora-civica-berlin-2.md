On July 16, 2017,  organized a new event called "DC (Why?) with Diaspora Civică Berlin".

On this occasion, we sought to make a recap of our activity coupled with a brainstorming session for future events and to attract new members.

More precisely, the meeting developed as follows: we talked in turn about ourselves, about the Diaspora Civică Berlin, and why each one of us joined the organization. We worked in teams welded by the affinity for a project format and the desire to put our ideas together and to generate better ones, and in the end, we all left excited about the thought of projects designed together.
