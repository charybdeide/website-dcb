Diaspora Civica Berlin invites you on Saturday, December 2 to resume pre-pandemic customs and traditions and celebrate Romani's national day.

📍 Location: Flutgraben e.V, Am Flutgraben 3, 12435 Berlin (barrier free)
🧩 The afternoon will start with a series of activities and workshops for parents and little ones, organised by our partners "[Biblioteca Prichindeilor](https://www.facebook.com/prichindeiberlin)" from 14:00 -18:00.
✨ And from 18:30 three well-known artists on the Berlin and Romanian art scene will turn the evening into a traditional neo-fusion.
🎶 [Dj Dokia](https://www.facebook.com/dokia3310) will add rhythm to the event, 🎨 and [#kitra ](https://www.facebook.com/kitrart)and [#passtrada](https://www.facebook.com/passtrada) will entertain the atmosphere with a live drawing act until late into the night.

🎟️ Admission: free.
🍹 Drinks and snacks will complement the relaxed atmosphere.
✅ Everyone is welcome

You can read more detailed descriptions of the artists and future updates on the [Facebook event](https://fb.me/e/1703iSzjF).
