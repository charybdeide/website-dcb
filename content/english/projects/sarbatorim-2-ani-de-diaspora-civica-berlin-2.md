On the 23rd of February 2019, the Diaspora Civică Berlin team celebrated together with friends and supporters 2 years of being active.

We wouldn't have managed to develop and organize the projects and events of the last two years without the ideas, support, and help of our volunteers, supporters, and partners.

We're grateful to all of them, which is why we wanted to meet and celebrate together.

We talked about future Diaspora Civică Berlin projects, elections, how we can bring a positive change in Romania, and other spontaneous subjects.
