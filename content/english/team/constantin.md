Hello, I'm Constantin. I grew up in Ploiesti and after high school I left Romania. I studied a mixture of languages, politics and economics in the UK, France, Spain and Germany.

Now I am passionate about software development and work in an IT firm in Berlin.

Still, I was unable to stay away from the struggle for social justice and democracy, so I am mainly active in the Diaspora Civica Berlin and Tafel Berlin.

I believe in DCB as a clotting force of the romanian Berlin diaspora at both community and critical mass levels. That critical mass of Romanians and Europeans prepared to act electorally and non-electorally to defend the ideals they share.
