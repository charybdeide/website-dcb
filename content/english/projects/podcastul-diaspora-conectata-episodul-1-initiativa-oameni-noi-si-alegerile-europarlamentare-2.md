For the first podcast episode created by Diaspora Civică Berlin, we talked to Mihai Polițeanu from the 'Inițiativa România' (Initiative Romania) about the initiative 'Oameni Noi' (New People),  the Romanian electoral system, and the importance of the European Parliamentary elections. You can listen to the podcast on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/user-531679124/diaspora-conectata-episodul-1-initiativa-oameni-noi-si-alegerile). 

This episode was part of the information campaign for the European Parliamentary elections on the 26th of May 2019.
