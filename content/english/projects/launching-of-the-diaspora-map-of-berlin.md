The Diaspora Map of Berlin is a project that aims to gather Romanian-speaking locations, as well as services of interest to the Romanian community in Berlin. On the map, you can find, for example, medical or law offices, translators and accountants, institutions and associations, as well as Romanian shops and restaurants. You can explore the map here, accessible on desktop and mobile: [https://ghid.diasporacivica.berlin/](https://ghid.diasporacivica.berlin/)
