On Sunday, May 23 2017, parents with children, educators, and Diaspora Civică Berlin members gathered in a beautiful kindergarten to exchange thoughts, opinions, and impressions related to civic education, its importance in raising children and different ways through which civic activism can be taught, even at an early age.

The event entitled "DC for young and old" is our way of saying that the next generations must be even more civically involved than us, and this has as its first step a civic education started early and practiced often - either in school or in the family.
