With the end of summer approaching, Diaspora Civică Berlin invites you to enjoy some of the last rays of sunshine together, as part of the Romanian Social Club in Volkspark Friedrichshain. The picnic is taking place on the 26th of August 2018, starting at 1 pm.

Everyone can bring their own food and drinks. This is also a good opportunity to find out what kind of events we are planning for the next couple of months and to support our activities through small donations ( any donation is much appreciated).

As with our past events, we promise to bring a good mood, games, and topics of discussion. Together we can share our ideas, joys, and concerns ( especially around the recent events in Romania).

For any new information, follow the [Facebook page of the event](https://www.facebook.com/events/2087034868212532/) ( the exact location will be shared approximately one hour before the start of the event).

We are waiting for you!

Team Diaspora Civică Berlin
