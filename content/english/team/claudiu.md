Hello, my name is Claudiu I thank you for being curious to find out more about me. I am a graduate of an MSc in Diplomatic Studies at the Mediterranean Diplomatic Academy of Malta and a resident in Berlin since 2010, with short interruptions given that my professional steps took me to Vienna and Amsterdam.

I have experience in international organizations, in the field of management consultancy and, more recently, in the development of events and conferences.

And yet something was missing! I have always wanted to offer something back to the Romanian society I was shaped in and to contribute to its harmonious development around  a set of solid values.

That is why I I joined DCB where I discovered dedicated people with a lot of work power and the same set of ideas as mine.

Together, even though living abroad, we can make Romania a more beautiful and civically active country!
