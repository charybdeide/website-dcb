I’ve been living in Berlin for over 15 years and have come to feel at home in this city that constantly challenges one to discover it from a new perspective. With a background in public policy and a career at the intersection of education, technology, and social inclusion, I’m interested in how we can build more connected and supportive communities.
Through my involvement with DCB, I hope to contribute to a more visible and engaged Romanian diaspora community, where we can get to know each other better, support each other and co-create.
