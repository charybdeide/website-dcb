On 22 February we celebrated 7 years since the founding of DCB and kicked off the 2024 election year with two short films (about past presidential elections and other important elections that will take place outside Romania) and discussions on how we can prepare (as a community but also individually) for the 2024 election year, which will include European parliamentary elections, local, parliamentary and presidential elections.
