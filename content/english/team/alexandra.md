Hi! I’m Alexandra Ioan and I am passionate about civil society organisations that address complex social problems. For over 15 years I’ve been collaborating with Romanian and international NGOs on non-formal education programs and on strengthening civic engagement and democracy.

I’ve studied Communication & Public Relations and Public Administration management in Bucharest, after which I’ve moved to Berlin for a second master in Public Policies at the Hertie School of Governance. Because my interest for intersectional collaboration has increased even more throughout my studies, I’ve decided to continue with a PhD in Governance which led me all the way to Stanford University, where I researched social entrepreneurship in a broader context.

For me, Diaspora Civică Berlin was born out of a very constructive dynamic between exceptional, engaged people, truly preoccupied with the future of Romania, of Romanians at home or abroad, as well as with European and global social, economic and political issues.

We discovered that we can develop our ideas together in order to bring positive changes in the societies and communities we live in.
