Romanian Social Club - the picnic edition - took place on August 27th, 2017, and was the event that ended the summer.

As always, we were glad to see each other and to remind ourselves that our ideals for Romania are shared by many others and thus, they have all the chances to move from the idea stage to the reality stage.

The essential condition is to want and believe that we can.
