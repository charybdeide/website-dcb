For the presidential elections of 2019, the Romanian Parliament has taken a series of measures that aim to prevent the long waiting times experienced during the European Parliament Elections. 

 	- The voting process for postal voting has been simplified and modernized.
 	- Voting outside of the Romanian borders will take place over 3 days, from Friday to Sunday. 

In preparation of the elections, the Diaspora Civică Berlin team has worked on a series of informational materials and activities which can help:

 	- **Explanations of the administrative steps which you must follow in order to be able to vote abroad: [https://diasporacivica.com/tot-ce-trebuie-sa-stii-despre-alegerile-prezidentiale-2019-pentru-diaspora/](https://diasporacivica.com/tot-ce-trebuie-sa-stii-despre-alegerile-prezidentiale-2019-pentru-diaspora/)**

 	- Information encouraging the postal voting ( administrative steps, deadlines, FAQ, centralizing the information from a variety of sources).
 	- Information and registrations session for the postal voting ( plus live streaming): [https://www.facebook.com/diasporacivicaberlin/videos/1267642356750390/](https://www.facebook.com/diasporacivicaberlin/videos/1267642356750390/) 
 	- Participation in the voting process as members of the voting committee at the Romanian Embassy in Berlin 
 	- Real-time information about the voter turn-out in Berlin, Germany, and the rest of the world during the two presidential voting rounds. 

You can find more information on our [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/diasporacivicaberlin/).
