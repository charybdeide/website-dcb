Hello, I'm Aleca and I consider myself to be many things – an architect, dreamer, organized and optimistic… ah, do not forget, funny, of course!

After 2 years of Graphic Design and Product Design in Bucharest, I turned to the study of architecture in Hanover. Over the years, I have enjoyed art, photography and art theory, a field in which we have had several projects. For over 6 years I have been working in Berlin where I hope to save the world of poor quality architecture and to improve the quality of life through innovation but especially through worthy living spaces that offer a + to life.

Diaspora Civica Berlin is for me the place where I found a community of people with whom I channel my forces and get involved in solving some of the problems facing Romanian society today. Together we build!
