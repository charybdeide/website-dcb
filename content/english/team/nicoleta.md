Hi, I'm Nicoleta! I came to Germany in 2015 and I graduated the Masters of Ethics, Politics and Economy at the Ruhr University Bochum.

During my studies I worked in the gastronomy industry where I saw the working conditions that many Romanian (and not only) new-comers in Germany have to deal with. This motivated me to write my masters thesis about the social working conditions of Romanian migrants in Germany. At the moment I work for a non-profit organization in Berlin where I'm involved in informing Romanian employees in Germany about the rights that they have here.

In Berlin I discovered the wonderful DCB team which helps me stay up to date with the latest political events in Romania. Together we try to encourage the Romanian diaspora in Germany to get civically engaged.

Although we don't live in Romania at the moment, we can contribute to fighting corruption simply by knowing and exercising our civic rights.
